july 12, 2026

A working list of formative essays and blogs that have shaped my values and thinking.

Criteria for inclusion (which I admit somewhat confound each other) include that they (1) challenge my assumptions, (2) influence my thinking/worldview/perspective in a discernible way, and (3) present a thoughtful and concise write-up of the author's thoughts.
